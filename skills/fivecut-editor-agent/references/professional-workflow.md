# Professional editing workflow

## Selection

Evaluate content relevance, performance, focus, exposure, framing, motion,
audio clarity, continuity, and emotional value. Prefer the strongest complete
thought, then use cutaways to conceal necessary dialogue tightening. Do not
remove pauses merely because a silence detector found them.

## Pacing

Match cut density to information and emotion. Let complex ideas breathe. Cut
faster during setup, repetition, action, or rhythmic montage. Avoid cuts within
phonemes, blinks that become distracting, and reaction shots that land after
their cause.

## Audio

Prioritize intelligibility. Use short equal-power fades at edits, room tone
under dialogue gaps, conservative noise reduction, and music ducking that begins
before speech. Keep true peak below -1 dBTP. Use approximately -14 LUFS for
typical web delivery unless the user specifies another target.

## Captions

Time captions to audible speech, not shot boundaries. Use one or two balanced
lines, preserve phrases, avoid orphan words, and provide enough reading time.
Verify names and technical terms against available context.

## Visual polish

Match exposure and white balance before applying a look. Use transitions only
for a motivated change in time, place, mood, or graphical continuity. Use zooms
for emphasis or reframing, not at arbitrary intervals. Keep type, colors,
caption placement, motion curves, and effect intensity consistent.

## Final QC

Watch start to finish at normal speed, then inspect cuts frame-by-frame around
known risks. Confirm sync, captions, spelling, media relinks, effect boundaries,
audio edits, ending frames, duration, resolution, frame rate, codecs, and a full
decode of the exported file.
